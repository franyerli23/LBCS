About Us

Luperon Boat Cleaning Services is building a professional local team dedicated to reliable, high-quality cleaning for boat owners visiting or staying in beautiful Luperón Bay. We clean directly at marinas, docks, and anchorages using pressure washers, fresh water, and professional marine products designed for fiberglass, gelcoat, vinyl, aluminum, and stainless steel.
